About me

My name is Hanna Ivannikova.

I have lived in Cyprus for over 8 years. I have a background in mathematics, work in IT, and invest in international markets.

I don't sell 'magic buttons.' I teach you how to build a system.

My approach:

  • Strategy instead of chaos
  • Long-term thinking
  • ETFs and diversification
  • Discipline over emotion

My main goal is for you to:

  • Know how to build your own capital
  • Understand how not to depend on a state pension
  • Decide for yourself when and how to work

Who I help

🎯

Those who want to invest but don't know where to start

🛌

Those keeping money under the mattress

💹

Those who want to create passive income

🔓

Those who want to build capital that works for them

What we'll learn together

1

How to open a brokerage account

I'll teach you how to choose a reliable international broker and complete every step of opening an account.

2

How to build an investment strategy

I'll explain how people assess their investment horizon, desired financial outcome, risk level, and comfortable investment amount.

3

How to build a diversified portfolio

Using examples, I'll show criteria for evaluating ETFs and index funds — without chaotic purchases or hot tips.

4

How to create a regular investment plan

Strategy, discipline, automation — we'll explore how these principles work through examples.

5

How to calculate your future retirement

I'll show how to calculate hypothetical capital-building scenarios and your 'freedom number.'

First session — investment strategy
